Usaremos repetidamente \(\tau\sigma=\sigma^2\tau\), \(\sigma^3=e\) e \(\tau^2=e\).

  1. \[\tau\sigma^2=(\tau\sigma)\sigma=\sigma^2\tau\sigma=\sigma^2(\sigma^2\tau)=\sigma^4\tau=\sigma\tau.\]

  2. \[\tau(\sigma\tau)=(\tau\sigma)\tau=\sigma^2\tau^2=\sigma^2.\]

  3. \[(\sigma\tau)(\sigma\tau)=\sigma(\tau\sigma)\tau=\sigma(\sigma^2\tau)\tau=\sigma^3=e.\]

  4. Como \(\tau\sigma^2=\sigma\tau\),

    \[(\sigma\tau)(\sigma^2\tau)=\sigma(\tau\sigma^2)\tau=\sigma(\sigma\tau)\tau=\sigma^2.\]

O grupo não é comutativo: \(\tau\sigma=\sigma^2\tau\), e os elementos distintos listados no enunciado mostram que \(\sigma^2\tau\neq\sigma\tau\).
